What changed from round two

  • The serif is only for day titles. Summaries are sans at 17 px, with no underlines. Phrases still open their moment and darken while touched.
  • The agent strip shows only while an agent is acting, with a 48 dp Stop. Use “Agent acting” above to see it.
  • The month sits above a full-width week row. Tap the month for older days. Away from today, a Today button appears.
  • “Still open” comes after the day’s places and says exactly what is open. Places show their time span.
  • Rows show two lines of preview, the time sits on the title’s line, and app icons are calmer.
  • Selected tabs use a filled icon and a heavier label. Chat is titled Chat, the model is a small line above the composer, and sources are rows.
  • Routines have switches. Agents list what they last did, and each agent has its own page with what it can see, its connection and Remove.
  • The open Fold shows the list of days on the left and the whole day on the right.
