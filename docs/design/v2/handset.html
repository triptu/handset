My pick: A, with B's place rows on the inner screen only

A is the closest to Juno. One warm neutral, colour only in app icons and photos, and the day reads like a page: title, three sentences, then rows. The tiny week row makes yesterday one tap or one swipe away without a sheet, and it shows at a glance which days you can go back to. B's accent is useful for links and Send but pulls the eye off the content, and its place rows add a second kind of row to learn. C's index is calm, but today's moments sit one tap away, and the day page is the point of the app.
